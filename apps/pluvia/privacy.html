Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Pluvia: Story & Collage Maker is provided by Alemdar Labs. This policy explains how the Android app handles information when you create collages, stories and carousel posts.

Your photos and projects

Photos, videos, text and project edits that you select or create are processed on your device. Pluvia does not upload your project media to our servers for editing, advertising or AI training. Drafts, imported media and downloaded resources are stored locally. Exported files are saved to your device or sent to a destination you choose.

Photo access and sharing

Pluvia uses the system picker to access the photos, videos or files you select. Saving an export may use the device’s media library. You control applicable permissions in device settings. If you share a design to another app, that app receives the selected export and its own privacy policy applies.

Templates and technical information

Pluvia downloads its template catalog, preview images, fonts and other creative resources from our online content service. These requests reveal technical information to our hosting provider, Cloudflare, such as IP address, request time, requested resource, response status and network/device request information. Service and error logs help deliver content, troubleshoot faults and protect the service. These requests do not include your selected photos or project contents.

Subscriptions

If you purchase a Pluvia subscription through Google Play, Google processes payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Purchase identifiers, selected product, transaction or purchase-token information and subscription status may be processed to validate purchases, provide paid access, restore purchases and prevent fraud. We do not receive your full payment-card details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play.

Support

If you contact info@alemdarlabs.com, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send private photos or payment details unless they are necessary for the issue you want us to investigate.

Advertising, tracking and accounts

The current Android app does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use the Android advertising ID. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. The mobile editor does not require a Pluvia account and does not offer cloud synchronization of your projects.

Service providers and international processing

We use Cloudflare to host and deliver online content, Google Play for Android distribution and any purchases, and email services to handle support. Providers may process information on servers outside your country. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably needed to protect rights and prevent abuse. Transfers and processing remain subject to applicable data-protection requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local drafts and imported media remain until you remove them in the app or remove the app’s stored data; exported files remain in the destination you selected until you delete them there. Device backup settings may separately retain copies. Technical logs are kept for the period needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service, subject to the hosting provider’s retention settings. Support and purchase records are retained only as needed to handle requests, maintain entitlements, resolve disputes and meet legal recordkeeping duties. You can ask us to delete information held by us by emailing info@alemdarlabs.com. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription or erase records held independently by Google.

Your choices and rights

You can remove local projects, manage device permissions, cancel subscriptions through Google Play and contact us about access, correction or deletion of information held by us. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, request portability or complain to a data-protection authority. Where applicable, processing is based on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in security and support, compliance with legal obligations, or your consent when required.

Children and security

Pluvia is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Online content is transferred over HTTPS. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. For privacy questions or requests, contact Alemdar Labs at info@alemdarlabs.com.
