Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar Labs handles information when you use Vakitify, a prayer times and Islamic companion application for iOS and Android.

Effective date: July 17, 2026 Android ID: com.alemdarlabs.vakitify iOS App Store ID: 6757681010

Vakitify does not require an account. Core preferences and religious-use data such as bookmarks, reading progress, prayer settings, and dhikr counts are stored locally on your device. Location is used only when you choose location-based prayer times or Qibla features. Vakitify may show ads through Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your region and privacy choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Vakitify is provided by Alemdar Labs. In this policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” mean Alemdar Labs, and “Vakitify” or “the app” means the Vakitify mobile application.

Privacy contact: info@alemdarlabs.com

2. Information stored on your device

Vakitify stores information locally so the app can work reliably and remember your choices, including:

  • language, theme, calculation method, madhhab, notification, sound, and accessibility settings;
  • selected city or locally derived prayer-location preferences;
  • Quran bookmarks, reading progress, favorites, search history, and audio preferences;
  • dhikr counts, saved prayers or duas, and other user-selected religious content;
  • cached prayer times, Hijri calendar data, and content required for offline use;
  • purchase or entitlement status needed to provide optional premium features; and
  • privacy choices and local advertising frequency information.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage. Vakitify does not currently provide a user account or cloud-save service, so local app data is not uploaded to an Alemdar Labs account.

3. Device permissions and sensitive information

Location

If you choose automatic location, Vakitify may request device location to calculate local prayer times, determine the Qibla direction, select the relevant calendar information, or detect that your city has changed. You can instead choose a city manually where that option is available. Precise coordinates are used for the requested feature and are not used by Alemdar Labs to create an account profile.

Compass and motion sensors

The Qibla compass may use device heading, compass, and motion-sensor information. This information is processed on the device to display direction and calibration guidance.

Notifications and calendar-related features

With your permission, Vakitify schedules prayer, adhan, reminder, and religious-calendar notifications. Some platform versions may offer optional calendar integration. You can change these permissions in the app or device settings. Vakitify does not read your contacts.

4. Advertising and consent

Vakitify uses Google Mobile Ads, including AdMob, to display ads and optional rewarded ads. Depending on your region, consent choice, device settings, and advertising availability, an ad may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable. Google and the advertising partners disclosed in the consent message may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information;
  • device, app, operating-system, and advertising or pseudonymous identifiers available to the SDK;
  • ad requests, impressions, views, clicks, reward events, and other ad interactions;
  • diagnostic, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information; and
  • privacy and consent signals required to determine the permitted advertising mode.

On iOS, if Vakitify asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, your device choice controls whether the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be available for personalized advertising and measurement across other companies’ apps or websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the app and Google Mobile Ads must operate without that tracking permission. You can change the platform-level permission in iOS Settings, subject to Apple’s controls.

Google’s User Messaging Platform may request updated consent information when the app launches, show privacy messages configured for eligible regions, and provide a privacy-options entry point when required. Vakitify checks the applicable consent status before requesting ads. Declining consent does not remove access to core prayer-time or Quran features, but a particular ad or optional ad reward may be unavailable.

7. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • calculate prayer times and provide Qibla, Quran, calendar, reminder, and offline features;
  • save preferences, bookmarks, progress, counts, and privacy choices;
  • deliver and restore optional purchases or subscriptions;
  • display, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in connection with advertising;
  • respond to support and feedback messages;

  • protect users, Vakitify, our providers, and our rights.

8. When information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or disclosed in these situations:

  • Service providers: Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform process advertising requests and privacy choices. Store platforms process downloads and purchases.
  • User-directed actions: information goes to a destination you select when you use a share, feedback, email, calendar, or other operating-system feature.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or protect rights, safety, security, and service integrity.
  • Business changes: information may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

9. Your privacy choices

  • Location: choose automatic or manual location and change location permission in device settings.
  • Notifications and calendar: change optional permissions in app or device settings.
  • Advertising privacy: use the in-app privacy-options entry point when shown or required, plus the advertising controls provided by iOS, Android, or your Google account.
  • Local data: clear supported history inside the app, clear app storage, or delete the app.
  • Requests: email info@alemdarlabs.com for privacy questions or requests relating to information we control.

10. Data retention and deletion

Local Vakitify data stays on your device until you clear it through an available in-app control, clear app storage, delete the app, or the operating system removes it. Deleting the app or clearing storage may permanently remove preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, dhikr counts, and other local data.

Support and feedback records are retained as long as needed to answer the request and maintain legal, security, and business records. To request deletion of eligible information we control, email info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject “Vakitify Data Deletion”. Because Vakitify has no account, we may need enough information to locate and verify the relevant support record.

Advertising, consent, store, and purchase information controlled by Google or Apple is retained and deleted under the applicable provider’s policies and user controls.

11. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, information is processed as necessary to provide features you request, based on consent for location, notifications, device storage, personalized advertising, or other optional processing where consent is required, to comply with legal obligations, and for permitted legitimate interests such as contextual advertising, security, support, fraud prevention, and service reliability. You may withdraw consent for future processing through the applicable app, device, or privacy-message control.

12. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including app private storage, permission controls, and encrypted network transport where supported. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Alemdar Labs is based in Turkey. Providers such as Google and Apple may process information in other countries whose data-protection laws differ from those where you live. Where required, transfers are handled using legally recognized safeguards.

13. Children’s privacy

Vakitify is a general-audience religious utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Vakitify, our providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update its effective date. If a change materially affects your choices, we will provide additional notice where required.
