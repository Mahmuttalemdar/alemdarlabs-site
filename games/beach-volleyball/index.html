Beach Volleyball: GG

A 2D pixel art beach volleyball game by Alemdar Labs, a game and app studio based in Istanbul, Turkey.

App ID: com.alemdarlabs.beachvolleyball

About the game

Beach Volleyball: GG is being developed around 1v1 and 2v2 beach volleyball matches in a 2D pixel art style. Planned modes include single-player play and online multiplayer with human players and computer-controlled opponents.

The game is in development. Release dates and store availability have not been announced.

Player support

For help with the game or a privacy request, email support@alemdarlabs.com. Include the game name and your platform. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
