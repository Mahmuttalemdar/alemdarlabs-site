Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar Labs handles information when you use Bubble Popple, a mobile bubble-matching puzzle game for Android.

Effective date: July 19, 2026 App package: com.alemdarlabs.bubbleshooter

Bubble Popple does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, files, camera, or microphone. The current version does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, social login, cloud save, leaderboards, or in-app purchases. Gameplay progress and settings stay on your device.

1. Who we are

Bubble Popple is provided by Alemdar Labs. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" mean Alemdar Labs, and "Bubble Popple" or "the game" means the Bubble Popple mobile application.

Privacy contact: info@alemdarlabs.com

2. Information stored on your device

Bubble Popple stores limited information locally so the game can work and remember your choices:

  • language selection;
  • sound and music volume, mute status, and restore-volume settings;
  • the last rating value you selected inside the game;
  • best stars and best scores for levels and concepts;
  • level unlock and progression state derived from your completed levels; and
  • standard app files needed by the game engine and operating system.

This data is stored in the game's private app storage. It is not uploaded to an Alemdar Labs account or cloud-save service because Bubble Popple does not currently provide accounts or cloud save.

3. Permissions and device access

The current Android export is configured without internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, SMS, phone, external storage, Bluetooth, notification, vibration, or account permissions. Bubble Popple does not use these permissions to collect personal or sensitive data.

The game may still use ordinary platform and engine functionality needed to display graphics, play audio, receive touch input, and store local app data.

4. Information you choose to share

Support messages

If you contact us by email or another support channel, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, protect the game, and maintain necessary business records.

Reviews and store actions

If you choose to rate or review Bubble Popple through Google Play, Google handles the review flow under its own terms and privacy policy. Bubble Popple may open the Google Play listing when you choose a store review action, but the current game does not send your rating or message to Alemdar Labs from inside the app.

5. Third-party services

The current version of Bubble Popple does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, social login, cloud save, multiplayer, leaderboard, or billing SDKs.

Google may process information when you download, update, rate, review, or otherwise use Google Play. That processing is controlled by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • save local progress, scores, stars, settings, and language choices;
  • unlock levels and concepts based on completed gameplay;
  • operate game screens, audio settings, touch controls, and puzzle flow;
  • respond to support messages you choose to send outside the game; and
  • comply with law and protect users, the game, and our rights.

7. When information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information. Bubble Popple does not upload local gameplay progress to Alemdar Labs servers and does not currently use third-party SDKs that collect advertising, analytics, crash, or purchase data from the game.

Information may be disclosed only in these limited situations:

  • User-directed actions: information goes to Google or another destination only when you choose to use a store, review, email, or support channel outside the game.
  • Service providers: email, hosting, and business providers may process support messages or website traffic for Alemdar Labs under their own obligations and our instructions.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.
  • Business changes: information may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and notice requirements.

8. Your privacy choices

  • Local play data: delete the app or clear its storage through your device settings.
  • Settings: change language, sound, and music choices inside the game.
  • Store controls: manage Google Play reviews, account settings, and privacy controls through Google Play or your Google account.
  • Requests: email info@alemdarlabs.com for privacy questions or requests relating to information we control.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete Bubble Popple, clear app storage, or the operating system removes it. Because Bubble Popple does not currently provide accounts or cloud save, Alemdar Labs cannot remotely view, restore, or delete local progress stored only on your device.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to answer the request and maintain necessary legal, security, and business records. We may retain limited records when required by law or needed to prevent fraud and protect legal rights.

To request deletion of eligible support information we control, email info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject "Bubble Popple Data Deletion". We may ask for information reasonably necessary to find the record and verify the request.

10. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the game and requested features, based on your consent where required, to comply with legal obligations, and for legitimate interests such as support, security, maintaining business records, and protecting the game and our rights where those interests are permitted by law.

11. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information under our control. Local game data is stored in the app's private storage area. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Alemdar Labs is based in Turkey. If you contact us or use a provider outside your country, information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws. Where required, transfers are handled using legally recognized safeguards.

12. Children's privacy

Bubble Popple is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

13. Future services

If a future version of Bubble Popple adds advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, cloud save, leaderboards, social login, in-app purchases, or other online services, we will update this policy and any required store declarations before using those services in a released version.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Bubble Popple, our providers, store requirements, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update its effective date. If a change materially affects your choices, we will provide additional notice where required.
