Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar Labs handles information when you use Luminode, a mobile puzzle game for iOS and Android.

Effective date: July 6, 2026 App package: com.alemdarlabs.luminode

Luminode does not require an account, does not ask for your precise location, and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or files from the game. The game stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. Advertising is provided through Google Mobile Ads, which may process device and advertising data as described below.

Who we are

Luminode is provided by Alemdar Labs. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" mean Alemdar Labs. "Luminode" or "the game" means the Luminode mobile application.

Contact: info@alemdarlabs.com

Information handled by Luminode

Gameplay and settings stored on your device

Luminode stores the following information locally on your device so the game can work correctly:

  • level progress, unlocked levels, stars, best moves, and best times;
  • daily puzzle progress and streak-related game state;
  • sound, haptics, language, performance mode, and other game preferences;
  • ad display counters used to avoid showing interstitial ads too frequently.

This local gameplay data is not sent to Alemdar Labs by the game. It remains on your device unless you delete the app, clear app data, or your operating system removes it.

Device performance information

To decide whether your device should use the premium interface or a lightweight interface, Luminode may read technical device information such as device manufacturer, model, Android version, available memory, low-memory device status, supported CPU ABI, GPU renderer, GPU vendor, graphics version, OpenGL ES version, Vulkan support, and frame timing performance.

This information is used only for performance adaptation. The automatic performance result is stored locally on your device. We do not use this information to identify you, build a profile about you, or send it to Alemdar Labs from the game.

Information you send to us

If you contact Alemdar Labs by email or another support channel, we may receive your email address, message content, and any information you choose to include. We use this only to respond to your request and support the game.

Advertising and third-party services

Luminode uses Google Mobile Ads, including AdMob, to show ads and rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, app activity related to ads, and diagnostics. Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own policies, including:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising

Apple and Google may also process information when you download, install, update, review, or make purchases related to the game through their stores. Those activities are governed by the applicable store platform's policies.

How we use information

We use information described in this policy to:

  • save your progress and preferences;
  • operate puzzles, rewards, achievements, and game flow;
  • select a suitable interface and rendering quality for your device;
  • show ads and rewarded hint ads through Google Mobile Ads;
  • respond to support messages you send to us;
  • protect, debug, and improve Luminode.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your local gameplay progress with Alemdar Labs servers because the game does not currently operate a Luminode account or cloud-save service.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers where necessary to operate the game, most notably Google Mobile Ads for advertising. We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or services.

Your choices

  • You can delete local gameplay data by deleting the app or clearing app data through your device settings.
  • You can change sound, haptics, language, and performance mode inside the game settings.
  • You can manage advertising personalization and reset your advertising identifier in your device or Google settings.
  • You can contact us at info@alemdarlabs.com for privacy questions or requests.

Data deletion

Luminode does not currently provide user accounts or cloud save. Gameplay progress, achievements, settings, and performance preferences are stored locally on your device. You can delete this local data by deleting Luminode from your device or clearing the app's data in your device settings.

If you have contacted Alemdar Labs by email or another support channel and want us to delete support messages or related contact information we may hold, send a request to info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject "Luminode Data Deletion". We may need enough information to identify the request and will delete eligible records unless we are required to keep them for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate business reasons.

Advertising data processed by Google Mobile Ads is controlled by Google and its advertising partners. You can manage advertising personalization and reset your advertising identifier through your device, Google, or Apple privacy settings.

Children's privacy

Luminode is a general audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information under our control. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Local gameplay data stays on your device until you delete the app, clear app data, or the operating system removes it. Support emails are retained as long as needed to handle your request and maintain business records. Third-party advertising data is retained according to the applicable third party's policies.

International users

If you use Luminode outside Turkey, your information may be processed in countries other than where you live, including where our service providers operate. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from your local laws.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Luminode changes or legal requirements change. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Continued use of the game after an update means the updated policy applies.
