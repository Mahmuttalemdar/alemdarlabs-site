Okey Connected

Delete your account and data

Request deletion of your Okey Connected account and associated data held by Alemdar Labs. You can make a request even if you have uninstalled the game.

Updated: September 11, 2026 App package: com.alemdarlabs.okeyconnected

Send a deletion request

Email info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject Okey Connected Account and Data Deletion. You do not need to reinstall the game to contact us.

Request deletion by email

The button opens a draft in your email app. Review and send the email to submit your request. If no email app opens, copy the address and subject into your preferred email service.

What to include

  • State that your request is for Okey Connected by Alemdar Labs.
  • If you used sign-in, tell us which method you used, such as Google, Apple, Steam, or Epic Games.
  • Include your Okey Connected player ID if you already know it. If you do not, contact us anyway; you do not need to reinstall the game to begin your request.
  • You may include an account email address or display name to help us locate the account, if that information is available to you.

We will explain any additional information needed to locate the relevant records and verify ownership. A display name or player ID alone is not proof of ownership. If you used a private or relay email address, or no longer have access to an earlier email address, let us know so we can discuss another appropriate verification method.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, recovery codes, access tokens, or payment card details. We do not need your Google, Apple, Steam, or Epic Games password.

What we delete

After verifying a valid request, we delete the Okey Connected account and associated personal data under our control, subject to the limited retention exceptions below. Where you have used online features, this includes:

  • your Okey Connected online profile, account records, and account-linked customization choices;
  • cloud saves, saved progress, preferences, virtual balances, and inventory associated with that account;
  • identifiable match history, replay records, statistics, achievements, and leaderboard data associated with that account and held under our control;
  • the account links and identifiers used specifically to provide Okey Connected's online features; and
  • related data held by service providers on our behalf, including Epic Online Services where used for Okey Connected. We request deletion of the relevant provider-held data as part of handling your request.

Deletion is permanent once completed. Your deleted online progress cannot be restored by signing in again. This request concerns Okey Connected; it does not delete your general Google, Apple, Steam, or Epic Games account, or accounts you use in other games.

Local data and guest play

To remove progress, settings, caches, or replays stored on your device, clear Okey Connected's app storage through your device settings or uninstall the game. Device menus vary by platform and manufacturer. We cannot remotely erase files stored only on your device. If your device provider keeps a backup, manage that copy through its backup settings.

Playing as a guest does not necessarily mean that all your data stays on the device. A guest identity may also have records associated with online services. Clearing local storage, signing out, or unlinking a sign-in provider does not automatically delete those records. Signing in again may restore existing cloud data if it has not been deleted.

If you want your online account and associated data deleted, send a request using the email above. If you played as a guest or do not know your player ID, contact us anyway; we will explain what information is needed to identify the relevant records and verify ownership. You do not need to reinstall the game to begin your request. You can also contact us about support correspondence or other personal information we hold.

Processing time and retained information

We will respond without undue delay and within the time limits required by applicable law. We will explain any verification needed, tell you the expected completion date, and confirm when your request has been completed. If a permitted delay is necessary, we will explain the reason and the revised timeline.

We may retain limited information where required by law or necessary for permitted security, fraud-prevention, or legal-claims purposes. A minimal record of your request and its outcome may also be retained to document how it was handled. We will explain any applicable retained categories, the reason for retaining them, and their retention period when responding. Such retention is not used to keep your deleted game profile active.

For more about local data, support correspondence, and your rights, see the Okey Connected Privacy Policy.
