Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar Labs handles information when you use Okey Connected, a digital Okey tile game with online multiplayer and computer-controlled opponents.

Effective date: October 28, 2026 App ID: com.alemdarlabs.okeyconnected

Okey Connected stores game information on your device and uses online services for features such as sign-in, multiplayer, and cloud saves. The information processed depends on your platform and the features you use. You can request deletion of your game profile and associated personal data through the account and data deletion process below.

1. Who we are

Okey Connected is provided by Alemdar Labs Ltd, trading as Alemdar Labs, in Istanbul, Turkey. In this policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” mean Alemdar Labs. We are responsible for personal information we control in connection with the game.

Privacy contact: info@alemdarlabs.com

2. Accounts and sign-in

Online features use Epic Online Services and the sign-in options available on your platform. Depending on the option you choose, these may include an Epic Games, Google, Apple, or Steam account, or a device-linked guest identity. Availability varies by platform and game version.

Sign-in may process a platform user identifier, an Epic Online Services product user identifier, authentication tokens, and profile details such as your display name or avatar. If a provider supplies an email address with your authorization, it is optional account information, not our primary player identifier. Where a verified address is needed for an account notice or a reply, an Apple private relay address can be used. We do not require a separate email address to play, merge accounts merely because their email addresses match, or use an account address for marketing without any separately required permission. Your sign-in provider handles its own password and authentication checks. Do not send passwords or authentication codes to Alemdar Labs support.

A guest identity may still be associated with a device and online service records. Guest play does not necessarily mean that all information stays on your device. Signing out or unlinking a provider does not, by itself, delete existing game records.

3. Game data and cloud saves

Depending on the features you use, the game stores and processes:

  • profile details you provide, such as a display name, biography, country, and language;
  • selected avatars, frames, titles, tile sets, table themes, and other customization choices;
  • sound, display, accessibility, tile-sorting, and social preferences;
  • game progress, match results and history, statistics, achievements, and leaderboard entries;
  • virtual chip and item balances, inventory, earned rewards, and related transaction history; and
  • saved match state or replay data, including player identifiers, moves, scores, and timing.

Settings and cached player data are stored locally. When online storage is available and your profile is connected, eligible profile, progress, inventory, and related records can also be synchronized through cloud storage. Clearing local storage does not delete corresponding cloud records, and signing in again may restore synchronized information.

4. Multiplayer and social features

Matchmaking, lobbies, invitations, reconnection, and multiplayer sessions process player and session identifiers, connection status, gameplay actions, and technical connection information. Online services may process your IP address and network details to establish and maintain the connection. A direct connection between players may also make an IP address available to the other connected device.

Other players may see your display name, avatar, public profile details, online status, and the game actions and results visible in the mode you join. Rankings and achievements may be visible where those features are available. Friends, invitations, and blocking features process the identifiers and relationship information needed to carry out your choices.

If you use communication features, messages or reactions you send are shared with the intended participants. Where voice chat is available and you enable microphone access, your voice is transmitted through the voice service to other participants. Manage microphone access in your device settings and use mute controls where available. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public profiles or conversations.

5. Technical information and support

The game and its online providers process technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service. This can include app and operating-system versions, device type, connection errors, timestamps, service identifiers, and diagnostic logs. Local logs or replays may contain player or session identifiers; consider their contents before attaching them to a support request.

If you contact us, we receive your contact details, message, and any screenshots, logs, or other attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Stores and operating systems may separately provide crash or performance information according to their own settings and policies.

6. Stores, purchases, and advertising

The store from which you obtain Okey Connected handles downloads, updates, reviews, and billing under its own policies. Where an optional purchase is offered, the store or payment provider processes payment details. We may process the product identifier, purchase status, transaction identifier, or receipt needed to deliver, validate, or restore a purchase and handle refunds or fraud. We do not receive your full payment-card details through store billing.

Optional rewarded ads

Where rewarded ads are available in your mobile version of Okey Connected, we use Google AdMob to offer an optional ad in exchange for the virtual reward described in the game. You choose whether to request an ad and can continue playing without doing so. Ad availability varies by platform, version, connection, and applicable privacy choices. These advertising practices apply when you use a version and feature that requests ads, not to versions without advertising.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address and an approximate location inferred from it; advertising, app, device, and applicable account-related identifiers; ad requests and interactions such as views and taps; and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses and shares this information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. Processing and personalization depend on your device settings, consent choices, region, and the ad service used. The SDK may use device storage or identifiers for these purposes.

To verify an earned reward, the game passes Google a reward reference that our server associates with your game account. Google's verification response includes the advertising transaction, reward details, and timing. Our servers use these records to verify eligibility, deliver rewards, enforce limits, and prevent the same reward from being credited twice. We do not include your email address, password, private messages, or voice content in that reward reference.

Where required, we obtain consent before requesting ads. Google's User Messaging Platform helps present applicable privacy messages and record your choices. You can reopen the applicable advertising privacy choices through the game's privacy controls. Declining personalized ads does not mean that every form of data processing stops: ads permitted by your choices may still need information for delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

On Android, you can also reset or delete the advertising identifier through your device's privacy settings. On Apple platforms, access to the advertising identifier for tracking requires the applicable system authorization. Declining a tracking request does not prevent core gameplay. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

10. Account and data deletion

For the dedicated request page and a pre-filled email, visit Delete your account and data. You can begin your request there even if you have uninstalled Okey Connected or no longer have access to the game.

To request deletion of your Okey Connected player profile and associated personal data we control, email info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject “Okey Connected Account and Data Deletion”. You can make this request without reinstalling the game. Include your platform, display name, and player identifier if available, so we can locate the correct records. We may ask for proportionate verification before acting; do not include a password, sign-in token, or full payment-card number.

A verified request covers eligible profile, cloud-save, and related identifiable gameplay records under our control. Deletion can remove progress, inventory, and access to associated features. Limited records may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, disputes, or the establishment or defense of legal claims. We will explain any applicable exception when responding, within the time required by applicable law.

You can remove local data by clearing app storage or deleting the game, subject to your device's backup settings. Local deletion, signing out, or unlinking an account does not automatically delete server-side records. Deleting Okey Connected data does not delete your Epic, Google, Apple, Steam, or other platform account; contact that provider for information it controls independently.

11. Retention

Profile, progress, virtual balances, item ownership, and the records needed to validate rewards are retained while needed to provide the account and requested features. Reward and ownership records are not treated as temporary diagnostic logs: deleting them prematurely could remove an earned item or allow the same reward to be credited again.

  • Short-lived social command records are kept for seven days and social notifications for 30 days. Pending invitations and cooldowns have their own expiry times.
  • If you enable local chat history, its cache is limited to seven days and 1,000 messages. This does not mean that other participants' devices or a provider's independent records are remotely erased at the same time.
  • Support and security records are kept for the purpose for which they are needed, with narrowly limited retention where a legal obligation or an actual dispute requires it.
  • Turkish rules require records of deletion, destruction, or anonymisation operations to be kept for at least three years. This is a minimal operation record, not a requirement to retain your entire deleted game profile, balances, messages, or authentication tokens.

Backup recovery cycles and the independent policies of service providers also affect when remaining copies expire. A restored backup must not reactivate an account whose deletion has been completed. Signing out, closing an ad, or clearing a device cache does not erase server records. See section 10 for account deletion and retention exceptions.
