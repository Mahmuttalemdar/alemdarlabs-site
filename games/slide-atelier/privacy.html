Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar Labs handles information when you use Slide Atelier, a mobile sliding puzzle game for iOS and Android.

Effective date: July 17, 2026 App ID: com.alemdarlabs.slideatelier

Slide Atelier does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. Most game data stays on your device. Usage analytics and crash diagnostics are off by default and are sent only if you enable the corresponding choice in the game. The game may show contextual, non-personalized ads through Google Mobile Ads after any required privacy choices are collected. We do not sell personal information or ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

1. Who we are

Slide Atelier is provided by Alemdar Labs. In this policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” mean Alemdar Labs, and “Slide Atelier” or “the game” means the Slide Atelier mobile application.

Privacy contact: info@alemdarlabs.com

2. Information stored on your device

Slide Atelier stores information locally so the game can function offline and remember your choices:

  • game settings, accessibility preferences, analytics and diagnostics choices, and advertising privacy status;
  • Journey progress, puzzle results, best scores, achievements, seals, and tutorial status;
  • locally earned game tokens, unlocked themes, equipped themes, and reward history;
  • Daily Puzzle attempts, results, streaks, and locally cached Daily content;
  • the current puzzle snapshot and session state used to resume interrupted play;
  • bundled or cached content and configuration needed for reliable offline play; and
  • local advertising frequency counters used to limit how often ads may appear.

This data is stored in the game’s private app storage. It is not uploaded to an Alemdar Labs account or cloud-save service because Slide Atelier does not currently provide accounts or cloud save.

3. Information processed through Firebase

Slide Atelier uses selected Google Firebase services. Firebase is configured to use secure network connections. Information may be processed on Google infrastructure in countries other than your own.

Remote Config

Firebase Remote Config helps us deliver safety switches and feature configuration. It may process a pseudonymous Firebase installation ID and technical request information such as the app identifier, app version, platform, and IP address. Remote Config is used for app functionality, not advertising. If it is unavailable, the game continues with bundled safe defaults.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics are disabled until you turn on Usage analytics in the game’s Privacy Choices screen. If enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase may process:

  • pseudonymous app-instance and device identifiers made available by the platform or SDK;
  • app version, operating system, device category or model, language, and session information;
  • approximate geographic information derived from an IP address; and
  • product interactions such as opening a screen, starting or completing a puzzle, using a feature, or changing a consent choice.

Analytics events do not include your name, email address, puzzle board cells, solution, deterministic seed, move history, purchase receipt, or free-form text. We do not set an account user ID because the game has no user accounts. We do not use analytics for cross-app tracking or personalized advertising.

Optional crash diagnostics

Crash diagnostics are disabled until you turn on Crash diagnostics in Privacy Choices. If enabled, Firebase Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, exception and diagnostic codes, timestamps, app and build version, operating system and device model, memory or storage state, and pseudonymous installation identifiers. These details help us identify and repair reliability problems. We do not intentionally include puzzle contents, receipts, contact information, or free-form user text in diagnostic reports.

4. Information you choose to share

Share cards

When you choose to share a Daily Puzzle result, Slide Atelier creates an image and result text on your device and opens the operating system’s share sheet. The content is sent only to the app or person you select. Alemdar Labs does not receive a copy through this feature. The selected destination handles the content under its own privacy terms.

Support messages

If you contact us by email or another support channel, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, protect the game, and maintain necessary business records.

5. App stores and optional purchases

Apple and Google process information when you download, update, review, or otherwise use their stores. Their processing is controlled by their own terms and privacy policies.

If a version of Slide Atelier offers optional in-app purchases, Apple or Google processes the payment. We do not receive your full payment-card details. We may receive a product identifier, purchase status, and a transaction or receipt token needed to validate the purchase, deliver the digital item, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and handle refunds or revocations.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • save local progress, preferences, and privacy choices;
  • operate puzzles, rewards, themes, Daily content, and resume functionality;
  • provide safe configuration and disable a feature during an operational incident;
  • understand game usage when analytics has been enabled;
  • detect, diagnose, and repair crashes when diagnostics has been enabled;
  • request and display contextual, non-personalized ads, deliver earned hint rewards, limit ad frequency, measure ad delivery, and prevent advertising fraud;
  • validate and restore optional purchases if they are offered; and
  • respond to messages, comply with law, and protect users, the game, and our rights.

7. When information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information. Our ad requests are configured without personalized targeting, and the game does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Google and its advertising partners may nevertheless process the limited advertising information described in Section 13 to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in connection with ads. Information may otherwise be disclosed only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers: Google Firebase processes the functional, optional analytics, and optional diagnostics information described above. Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform process advertising requests and privacy choices as described in Section 13.
  • User-directed sharing: information goes to a destination you select through the operating system share sheet.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.
  • Business changes: information may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and notice requirements.

We require service providers handling data on our behalf to protect it consistently with their contracts, applicable law, and the commitments in this policy.

8. Your privacy choices

  • Usage analytics: turn it on or off at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • Crash diagnostics: turn it on or off independently in Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • Advertising privacy choices: where required, open Settings > Privacy Choices > Advertising privacy choices to review or change choices collected through Google’s User Messaging Platform.
  • Device advertising controls: you may also use the privacy and advertising controls provided by iOS, Android, or your Google account.
  • Local play data: use Delete local play data in Privacy Choices.
  • All local app data: delete the app or clear its storage through device settings.
  • Requests: email info@alemdarlabs.com for privacy questions or requests relating to information we control.

Analytics, diagnostics, and advertising choices are separate. Turning off analytics or diagnostics stops future collection by that service from the game. Declining an advertising choice may result in limited ads, no available ad, or an unavailable rewarded hint, depending on your region and Google’s requirements. It does not block core gameplay or your ability to delete local play data.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local play data remains on your device until you use the in-game deletion control, clear app storage, delete the game, or the operating system removes it. The in-game deletion control removes profile-linked progress, puzzle results and snapshots, economy and Daily records, pending event records, experiment assignments, and best scores. Privacy choices and essential app settings are retained so deletion does not silently turn collection back on and the game can continue to work offline.

Optional analytics data is retained according to the Google Analytics retention configuration and is deleted or aggregated when it is no longer needed for the purposes described here. Firebase states that Crashlytics keeps crash reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal. Firebase installation identifiers used for functional services are handled under Firebase’s published retention and deletion practices.

Google retains advertising, consent, and fraud-prevention information according to its own policies and legal obligations. Advertising privacy choices can be reviewed through the in-game privacy entry point when required, and device-level advertising controls are available through your operating system or Google settings. Alemdar Labs cannot directly identify or delete information controlled independently by Google without a corresponding identifier or legal basis.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to answer the request and maintain necessary legal, security, and business records. We may retain limited records when required by law or needed to prevent fraud and protect legal rights.

To request deletion of eligible support or remotely processed information we control, email info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject “Slide Atelier Data Deletion”. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to find the record and verify the request. Because Slide Atelier has no account, we may not be able to connect a remote pseudonymous record to you without an identifier that matches that record.

10. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the game and its requested features, based on your consent for optional analytics, crash diagnostics, device storage, or advertising processing where consent is required, to comply with legal obligations, and for legitimate interests such as security, support, contextual advertising, fraud prevention, and improving reliability where those interests are permitted by law. You may withdraw consent for future optional collection at any time through the applicable in-game control without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

11. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including app private storage, collection controls, limited diagnostic fields, and encrypted network transport for Firebase and Google Mobile Ads services. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Alemdar Labs is based in Turkey, and providers such as Google may process information in other countries. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, transfers are handled using legally recognized safeguards.

12. Children’s privacy

Slide Atelier is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

13. Advertising and tracking

Slide Atelier uses Google Mobile Ads, including AdMob, for optional rewarded hint ads and limited interstitial ads shown at natural breaks after puzzle results. Rewarded ads are initiated by your choice. Interstitial ads are subject to local frequency limits and operational controls. If an ad is unavailable, dismissed, or fails, core gameplay and navigation continue without requiring the ad.

Ad requests from the game are configured as non-personalized. Slide Atelier does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission and does not request access to the iOS advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. Non-personalized ads still use contextual and limited technical information. Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information;
  • device, app, operating-system, and pseudonymous device or app identifiers available to the SDK;
  • ad requests, impressions, views, clicks, reward events, and other interactions with ads;
  • diagnostic, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information; and
  • privacy and consent signals required to determine whether an ad request is permitted.

Slide Atelier uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request updated consent information when the app launches, present applicable privacy messages configured for the app, and provide a privacy-options entry point when required. The game checks whether Google permits ad requests before loading an ad. Your available choices and the type of ad that may be served can vary by region, law, device settings, and the choice you make.
