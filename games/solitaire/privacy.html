Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar Labs handles information when you use Solitaire, a collection of single-player card games for Android, iOS, and macOS: Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid, and TriPeaks.

Effective date: September 27, 2026 App identifier: com.alemdarlabs.solitaire

Ordinary Solitaire games can be played offline without signing in. Solitaire includes an optional, invitation-only Klondike playtest. Only after you read the notice and agree does that test create a random test identity and send its results to Alemdar Labs through Google Firebase. Ordinary saved games are not automatically uploaded. The game does not serve ads, offer in-app purchases, or use automatic crash-reporting services.

1. Who we are

Solitaire is provided by Alemdar Labs. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" mean Alemdar Labs, and "Solitaire" or "the game" means the Solitaire application on supported mobile and desktop platforms.

Privacy contact: info@alemdarlabs.com

2. Information stored on your device

Solitaire stores local information to resume your games and remember your choices, including:

  • saved hands for Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid, and TriPeaks, including card positions and deal identifiers;
  • move history, score, elapsed play time, and other state needed to resume a hand or undo moves;
  • new-game preferences such as difficulty, Klondike draw and scoring rules, and Spider suit count;
  • language, card appearance, display, audio, input, and accessibility preferences, where available; and
  • local recovery records, settings files, and ordinary files needed by the game engine.

This information is stored in app-specific storage on your device. Ordinary games do not use an online profile or cloud-save service. Optional playtest records are kept separately as described below. Your operating system or device provider may separately offer backup or device-transfer features under its own settings and policies.

3. Permissions and device access

The game uses internet access for the optional playtest's authentication and result delivery. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, calendar, SMS, advertising ID, or shared photo and file storage permissions. Ordinary play does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or contact list.

On Android, the package declares permissions for checking network status and Google Play licensing, keeping the device awake, adjusting audio settings, and vibration. It also declares an app-specific permission that protects internal messages between app components. These permissions support platform and engine functionality; they are not used to collect your contacts, location, or media.

Google Play, Apple's App Store and TestFlight, and your device may use their own network access to install, update, or deliver game files. That platform activity is separate from the game's local saved hands and preferences.

4. Information you choose to share

Invitation-only playtesting

Joining requires an invitation and your explicit agreement to the in-app notice. We collect the random test identity, assigned test plan and hand identifiers, game and app version, move history, result, active play time, use of Hint, Solver and Undo, difficulty ratings, and any comment you choose to write. Please do not put personal or sensitive information in comments.

We use these records to operate the test, check game behavior, assess difficulty, and improve Solitaire. Results are first saved on your device. If you are offline, the app keeps them there and sends them when it is open with a connection. The app does not import ordinary saved games or earlier local tests into this upload. Test credentials are held in platform-protected storage.

Google Firebase Authentication supplies the random identity and Cloud Firestore stores test records on our behalf. Requests use HTTPS. These services receive the connection information needed to handle and protect requests, such as an IP address. Our Firestore database is in Frankfurt, Germany; authentication and service operations may involve other locations under Google's service terms. This is not a public profile or cross-device cloud-save feature.

Support messages

If you contact us by email or another support channel, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Screenshots, saved-game files, and device details are shared with us only if you choose to send them. Please avoid including unrelated personal or sensitive information.

Store reviews and website visits

If you rate or review Solitaire through Google Play or Apple's App Store, the relevant store provider handles that activity under its own terms and privacy policy. If you visit our website, our hosting provider may process ordinary request data such as your IP address, browser information, and access time to deliver and protect the website. Website information is also described in the Alemdar Labs Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

Apart from the optional Firebase playtest described above, the current game does not activate advertising, online analytics, automatic crash reporting, social login, cloud save, multiplayer, leaderboards, or store billing. It is built with Unreal Engine and includes platform libraries; bundled support for online services does not mean those features are offered in this version.

Google Play, Apple's App Store, and TestFlight may handle installation, updates, licensing, delivery of game files, reviews, and platform diagnostics. Google, Apple, and your device provider may process device, app, download, or diagnostic information under their own policies and your platform settings. We may receive aggregate store reports or platform-provided crash information to understand compatibility and fix problems.

For TestFlight beta testing, Apple makes tester information, usage, crash reports, and feedback available to us through App Store Connect under its TestFlight terms and privacy information. This platform-provided beta feedback is separate from the optional in-game Firebase playtest.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • save and resume hands, maintain scores and move history, and support Undo and local recovery;
  • remember game rules, difficulty, appearance, and other preferences;
  • run the card games and their on-device Hint and Solver features;
  • operate the optional playtest, review its results and improve game behavior and difficulty;
  • respond to support requests and investigate technical issues; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect users, the game, and our rights.

7. When information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Ordinary saved hands and preferences remain local; only the separate playtest data you agree to send is uploaded.

Information we receive outside local gameplay may be disclosed in these limited situations:

  • User-directed actions: information you choose to send through email, a store review, or another external support channel is handled by that channel and its recipients.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase processes the optional playtest's identity and records on our behalf. Email, hosting, and business providers may process support correspondence or website requests to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business changes: information we control may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and notice requirements.

8. Your privacy choices

  • Playtest data: open the Klondike playtest card on Home, or use Back during a test hand, then choose "Delete my test data" and confirm. This withdraws participation, deletes this test's server records and anonymous identity, and removes its local records. Ordinary games are preserved. A connection is needed to complete deletion; interrupted deletion resumes when the app is opened again.
  • Local game data: use the app-data removal controls available on your platform. On Android, you can clear app storage. Removing an application may leave saved files or settings, especially on macOS; contact us if you need help locating Solitaire's local data.
  • Game preferences: use the available settings and new-game options to change your choices.
  • Platform controls: manage reviews, diagnostics, backups, and device transfers through your Google or Apple account and device settings, where those controls are available.
  • Privacy requests: email info@alemdarlabs.com to ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we control, or exercise other rights available under applicable law.

9. Data retention and deletion

Identifiable playtest records are retained while needed to operate and evaluate the test, unless you delete them or make a verified deletion request. We remove them when no longer needed for that purpose. Local unsent results remain until delivered or deleted. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete server records and can remove the credential needed for self-service deletion. If the organizer has already downloaded a review copy, contact us or the organizer so that copy is also included in your deletion request.

To request deletion of your Solitaire account or personal data held by Alemdar Labs, visit Delete your account and data. You can contact us through that page even if you have uninstalled the game.

Local game data remains on your device until you replace it through gameplay, remove the app's stored data, or the operating system removes it. Removing only the application may leave saved files or settings, especially on macOS. Alemdar Labs cannot remotely view, restore, or delete saves stored only on your device. If your device provider keeps a backup, manage or delete that copy through its separate backup controls.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to answer your request and maintain necessary legal, security, and business records. We may retain limited records when required by law or needed to protect legal rights.

To request deletion of eligible support information we control, email info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject "Solitaire Data Deletion". We may ask for information reasonably necessary to locate the record and verify the request. If you joined a playtest, mention that and the participant label shown in the test screen if known. You do not need to reinstall the app to contact us.

10. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the game and requested features, based on your consent where required, to comply with legal obligations, and for legitimate interests such as support, security, maintaining business records, and protecting the game and our rights where those interests are permitted by law.

11. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information under our control. Local game data is stored in app-specific storage on your device. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Alemdar Labs is based in Turkey. If you contact us or use a provider outside your country, information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws. Where required, transfers are handled using legally recognized safeguards.

12. Children's privacy

Solitaire is a single-player card-game collection. The current game does not require players to submit personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support channels without any consent required by applicable law. Parents and guardians should supervise store, email, and other external services used by children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact info@alemdarlabs.com so we can review the request and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

We will provide any required notices and obtain consent where required.
