Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar Labs handles information when you use Solitaire, a collection of single-player card games for Android: Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid, and TriPeaks.

Effective date: September 11, 2026 App package: com.alemdarlabs.solitaire

Solitaire can be played offline and does not require an account. In version 0.1.2, game progress and settings are saved on your device. The game does not send your saved hands to Alemdar Labs, serve ads, offer in-app purchases, or use online analytics or automatic crash-reporting services.

1. Who we are

Solitaire is provided by Alemdar Labs. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" mean Alemdar Labs, and "Solitaire" or "the game" means the Solitaire mobile application.

Privacy contact: info@alemdarlabs.com

2. Information stored on your device

Solitaire stores local information to resume your games and remember your choices, including:

  • saved hands for Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid, and TriPeaks, including card positions and deal identifiers;
  • move history, score, elapsed play time, and other state needed to resume a hand or undo moves;
  • new-game preferences such as difficulty, Klondike draw and scoring rules, and Spider suit count;
  • language, card appearance, display, audio, input, and accessibility preferences, where available; and
  • local recovery records, settings files, and ordinary files needed by the game engine.

This information is stored in app-specific storage on your device. Solitaire does not currently provide an Alemdar Labs account, online profile, or cloud-save service. Your operating system or device provider may separately offer backup or device-transfer features under its own settings and policies.

3. Permissions and device access

The Android 0.1.2 package does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, calendar, SMS, advertising ID, or shared photo and file storage permissions. The game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or contact list to play.

The package declares permissions for checking network status and Google Play licensing, keeping the device awake, adjusting audio settings, and vibration. It also declares an app-specific permission that protects internal messages between app components. These permissions support platform and engine functionality; they are not used to collect your contacts, location, or media.

Google Play and your device may use their own network access to install, update, or deliver game files. That platform activity is separate from the game's local saved hands and preferences.

4. Information you choose to share

Support messages

If you contact us by email or another support channel, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Screenshots, saved-game files, and device details are shared with us only if you choose to send them. Please avoid including unrelated personal or sensitive information.

Store reviews and website visits

If you rate or review Solitaire through Google Play, Google handles that activity under its own terms and privacy policy. If you visit our website, our hosting provider may process ordinary request data such as your IP address, browser information, and access time to deliver and protect the website. Website information is also described in the Alemdar Labs Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

The current game does not activate advertising, online analytics, automatic crash reporting, social login, cloud save, multiplayer, leaderboards, or store billing. It is built with Unreal Engine and includes platform libraries; bundled support for online services does not mean those features are offered in this version.

Google Play may handle installation, updates, licensing, delivery of game files, reviews, and platform diagnostics. Google and your device provider may process device, app, download, or diagnostic information under their own policies and your platform settings. We may receive aggregate store reports or platform-provided crash information to understand compatibility and fix problems.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • save and resume hands, maintain scores and move history, and support Undo and local recovery;
  • remember game rules, difficulty, appearance, and other preferences;
  • run the card games and their on-device Hint and Solver features;
  • respond to support requests and investigate technical issues; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect users, the game, and our rights.

7. When information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information. Solitaire does not upload your local saved hands or preferences to Alemdar Labs servers or use them for targeted advertising.

Information we receive outside local gameplay may be disclosed in these limited situations:

  • User-directed actions: information you choose to send through email, a store review, or another external support channel is handled by that channel and its recipients.
  • Service providers: email, hosting, and business providers may process support correspondence or website requests to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business changes: information we control may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and notice requirements.

8. Your privacy choices

  • Local game data: clear Solitaire's app storage or uninstall the game through Android settings. This removes local progress and preferences.
  • Game preferences: use the available settings and new-game options to change your choices.
  • Platform controls: manage reviews, diagnostics, backups, and device transfers through your Google account or device settings, where those controls are available.
  • Privacy requests: email info@alemdarlabs.com to ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we control, or exercise other rights available under applicable law.

9. Data retention and deletion

To request deletion of your Solitaire account or personal data held by Alemdar Labs, visit Delete your account and data. You can contact us through that page even if you have uninstalled the game.

Local game data remains on your device until you replace it through gameplay, clear app storage, uninstall Solitaire, or the operating system removes it. Alemdar Labs cannot remotely view, restore, or delete saves stored only on your device. If your device provider keeps a backup, manage or delete that copy through its separate backup controls.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to answer your request and maintain necessary legal, security, and business records. We may retain limited records when required by law or needed to protect legal rights.

To request deletion of eligible support information we control, email info@alemdarlabs.com with the subject "Solitaire Data Deletion". We may ask for information reasonably necessary to locate the record and verify the request. The current game has no online account to delete.

10. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the game and requested features, based on your consent where required, to comply with legal obligations, and for legitimate interests such as support, security, maintaining business records, and protecting the game and our rights where those interests are permitted by law.

11. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information under our control. Local game data is stored in app-specific storage on your device. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Alemdar Labs is based in Turkey. If you contact us or use a provider outside your country, information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws. Where required, transfers are handled using legally recognized safeguards.

12. Children's privacy

Solitaire is a single-player card-game collection. The current game does not require players to submit personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support channels without any consent required by applicable law. Parents and guardians should supervise store, email, and other external services used by children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact info@alemdarlabs.com so we can review the request and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

If a future version enables advertising, online analytics, crash reporting, accounts, cloud save, leaderboards, purchases, or other online features, we will update this policy and the relevant store declarations before those services are used in a released version. We will provide any required notices and obtain consent where required.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Solitaire, our providers, store requirements, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update its effective date. If a change materially affects your choices, we will provide additional notice where required.
